# Mirall Trademark Policy

> **DRAFT — NOT LEGAL ADVICE.** Review with counsel. Trademark rights are much
> stronger once registered — consider registering "Mirall" (and the logo) in
> your key markets before launch.

The Mirall **source code** is free software, licensed under AGPL-3.0. The
Mirall **name, logo, and brand** are **not** covered by that license. Code is
licensed; trademarks are not.

This separation is deliberate: it lets anyone exercise their AGPL rights — fork,
modify, redistribute — while ensuring users can still trust that something
called "Mirall" really comes from us.

## What you may do (no permission needed)

- Use the name "Mirall" to refer to this project truthfully (nominative use):
  "compatible with Mirall", "a plugin for Mirall", "based on Mirall".
- Redistribute **unmodified** official builds under the name Mirall.
- Use the logo to link to the official project.

## What requires a different name

If you distribute a **modified** version of Mirall, you must **not** present it
under the "Mirall" name or logo in a way that could confuse users about its
origin. Give your fork its own name and branding. You may state, factually, that
it is "derived from Mirall" or "a fork of Mirall".

## What requires our written permission

- Using "Mirall" or the logo in a product name, company name, domain, or
  app-store listing.
- Using the branding in a way that implies official status, endorsement, or
  affiliation.
- Merchandising.
