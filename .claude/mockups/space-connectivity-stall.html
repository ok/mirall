Per-space connection-stall notice — mockup

Phase 0 UI delta from .claude/tasks/plan-phase0-stall-detector.md. Click the toggle to flip light/dark.

What's proposed vs. recreated

Proposed (the delta): the SpaceConnectivityNotice — a role="status" banner shown when the DHT is up but no peer has connected in this space past the grace window. Two contexts: the pending waiting view (§2) and the approved view above the files area (§3).
Recreated from code (baseline truth): the whole SpaceView — header, pending panel, empty-share state, and members box — reproduced from src/renderer/screens/SpaceView.tsx + MembersBox/MemberCard. Strings are the real locale values; stalled* keys are proposed new copy.

1 · The notice component (both variants)

A11y: lives in role="status" aria-live="polite" so it's announced on appear; status is in the text (not colour); the Retry button has an accessible name and a focus-visible ring. Built from tokens — surface / warning border / secondary-container pill — no new primitives.

Pending variant · recommended

Can't reach the space yet

You're connected to the network, but haven't reached anyone in this space yet. If this keeps happening, the network you're on may be blocking the connection.

Approved-member variant · recommended

Can't reach the space yet

You're online, but can't reach anyone in this space right now. If this keeps happening, the network you're on may be blocking direct connections.

Alternative treatment (louder) — shown for the decision; not recommended

Can't reach the space yet

You're connected to the network, but haven't reached anyone in this space yet. If this keeps happening, the network you're on may be blocking the connection.

Rationale for recommending the quiet treatment: the stall is recoverable and often transient, so a soft, informational surface (matching the app's quiet cards) is less alarming than a full warning panel — while still clearly actionable via Retry.

2 · Pending join, stalled primary case — the incident

You used an invite and are waiting to be let in, but no peer has connected (the inviter is offline, or the network blocks the connection). The notice appears below the existing "Waiting to be let in" copy, inside the same centred role="status" region (recreated from SpaceView.tsx:305-321).

3 · Approved member, can't reach anyone secondary case

You're an approved member with other members in the space, but none are reachable past the grace window. The notice spans the content width above the files+sidebar grid (same slot as the join-request banner). Gated on otherMembers > 0 so a freshly-created, not-yet-invited space never false-alarms. Reading the mockup

  • · Plan: .claude/tasks/plan-phase0-stall-detector.md (§4.8 component, §4.9 wire-in, §4.10 strings).
  • · Recreated from code: header (back / title / Cancel-request vs Invite+More), the pending panel (SpaceView.tsx:305-321), the empty-share state (:331-359), the members box (MembersBox.tsx / MemberCard.tsx). Strings verbatim from locales/en/common.json.
  • · Proposed copy (new space.stalled* keys): "Can't reach the space yet" · pending & member hints · "Try again". To be added to all five locales; German uses the generic masculine (no inclusive forms).
  • · Tokens: card = bg-surface-container-lowest + border-warning/50; Retry = the primary bg-primary / text-on-primary button with rounded-xl (same as the Invite / primary action buttons — near-black in light, the brand orange in dark); heading = text-on-surface, body = text-on-surface-variant. No raw hex except the avatar placeholders (Material avatars are approximated in mockups, as are the stroke icons).
  • · Icon-colour note: the wifi_off glyph is drawn in text-secondary rather than text-warning — pure warning-yellow reads too faint as a thin stroke on a near-white card. Flagged for the plan (it currently says text-warning). Confirm wifi_off exists in Material Symbols; else fall back to warning.
  • · A11y: role="status" aria-live="polite" announces appearance; the status text (not colour) carries meaning; Retry is a real <button> with an accessible name + focus-visible ring — targetable by the frontend suite (the s69 assertion). The member status dots stay aria-hidden; "Online"/"Offline" text is the accessible signal, unchanged.
  • · Out of scope (later phases): the global Network-Status verdict is untouched (Phase 1); per-member direct/relayed/unreachable badges (Phase 2); relay fallback (Phase 3).
