After a restore (backup folder or recovery key) the app opens at once instead of the blocking Restore screen. Everything you can read is there; only changes wait until someone you share with confirms nothing newer exists (the #565 fork protection). Plan: plan-restore-readonly-shell.md. What changes

Today

After “Restore”, a full-screen Restoring your profile page until someone you share a space with comes online. Your spaces and files are already on the device but hidden.

Proposed

The app opens with everything visible. A banner explains the check. Actions that would change your profile or your shared files are disabled with that reason. The progress, “Rejoin with invite” and “Start a new identity” live on Protection status. When the check passes, the banner goes and everything is enabled — no restart screen.

Still allowed during the check: browsing spaces, members and files; downloading; joining a space with an invite; settings; mirrors of others’ folders. Waits: creating spaces, inviting, approving or denying requests, leaving a space, sharing or removing files and folders, editing your name and picture.

Rules

  • Disabled = aria-disabled + one shared reason; never a request that fails. A race (the check state changed underneath) gets the same sentence as an error.
  • Leaving a space is blocked while your profile is unconfirmed: leaving every space would otherwise end the check without anyone confirming — the fork the check exists to prevent.
  • Join requests that arrive meanwhile are shown and answered once the check passes (approve/deny wait).
  • Files added to a shared folder meanwhile are published when that space is confirmed (the folder is rescanned).

Rejected

  • Keep the blocking screen: hides data that is already here, for a check that only concerns writes.
  • Hide the disabled actions: people would hunt for them; a visible, explained wait is clearer.
  • A modal on launch: the banner is enough and does not stop browsing.
