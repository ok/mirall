§2 — Proposed: one card, one line, the field

PROPOSED — the delta. Nothing new is invented: one existing card, prose demoted to plain muted body text on the panel, one box deleted outright.

The four edits

  1. One card, one row. Badge left, key right, copy button. The card drops to bg-surface-container-low — the ramp step design.md pairs with a modal panel (the panel is surface-container-lowest), the same fill FIELD_SURFACE already uses two rows below. Card and field now agree.
  2. kindNote deleted. “You were invited to this one” is the badge restated in a sentence. The badge is the note.
  3. Privacy note unboxed and cut to one sentence NEW copy. Plain text-sm text-on-surface-variant on the panel — the same treatment the step-1 description already gets from ModalHeader descriptionSize="sm". 55 words → 18. The aphorism (“Blind to your files is not blind to your contacts”) and the “lasting name” clause go; what survives is the only part that changes a decision.
  4. restartWarning box deleted — not moved. ReconnectNotice says it behind the modal, with the button that fixes it.

a11y: the card keeps its sr-only full key (relays.decodedKey) and the badge keeps srLabel = relays.kindFor, so the truncated key and the bare word “Private relay” still announce in full. Dropping the role="status" warning removes a live region that fired on a step the user navigated to deliberately — a live announcement for something they just caused.

§3 — The open-relay variant, and one rejected alternative

Same step for a key rather than an invite, plus the version that goes one cut too far.

Open relay — proposed

Add a relay

Open relay k4m2pqv1…8x3zra

An open relay has no privacy line: there is no lasting identity to disclose. The step is card + field, three elements total — which is what the step always should have been.

Rejected: drop the privacy line too

The most minimal version is card + field for both kinds, with the docs link on the settings page carrying everything else. Rejected, and the reason is specific: the docs explain how to run a relay. They do not tell the person accepting an invite that the operator gains a durable view of when they are online and who they talk to. That is the one fact on this screen that could change the answer, and the screen is the last moment to state it before the key is stored.

Cutting it to one sentence is the compromise: it stays, unboxed and unstyled, in the same voice as the step-1 description. If you'd rather cut it entirely, that is a one-line change — but it should be a deliberate call, not a side effect of tidying.

String changes

relays.privacyNote rewritten NEW; relays.kindNote.* and relays.restartWarning become unreferenced — deleted from all five locales, which is what test/invariants/i18n-unreferenced-keys.test.js requires. restartWarningRemove stays; it has its own consumer.

Suggestions

It is not a dark-mode-only bug

--color-tertiary-fixed is #ffdad9 light and #352840 dark — a pale pink and a near-black plum. Both are container fills being used as a foreground. Against the card (surface-container-low):

TokenDarkLight
tertiary-fixed (today)1.07:11.16:1
secondary (proposed)7.37:15.84:1
on-surface-variant (alt)10.02:18.44:1

The non-text floor is 3:1. Today's bulb misses it by a factor of three in both themes — it is invisible on your light build too, which is why this reads as a token picked once and never rendered.

Why secondary and not the neutral

design.md §Typography already assigns text-secondary to the eyebrow/hint role, and it is the warm gold the app uses for “here is a pointer, not a verdict” — including the close button in the modal above. on-surface-variant is the safer neutral and is listed as the fallback; it reads as body text rather than an affordance, which flattens the list into a grey wall.

Delete the token, don't repoint it

This icon is --color-tertiary-fixed's only consumer in the whole renderer, and the token is not in design.md. So the change is three edits, not one: the class, both declarations in styles/tailwind.css, and the mapping in tailwind.config.js — otherwise test/invariants/unused-color-tokens.test.js goes red on the orphan.
