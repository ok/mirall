Backup & recovery

Where your backup lives, your recovery key, and restoring.

Backup folder

Mirall backs up after changes, every hour and when you quit, and keeps recent backups while thinning out older ones. Best on another drive or a network folder.

/Volumes/Backup

Recovery key

Passphrase

Opens your recovery key. Changing it makes a new key; copies you saved elsewhere keep the old passphrase.

Remind me to check my passphrase

Twice a year, Mirall asks you to type it once — nothing is sent anywhere.

Restoring on a new computer

  1. Install Mirall and open it.
  2. Choose Already used Mirall? Restore your account.
  3. Pick your backup folder and enter your passphrase. Your recovery key is in the folder.

No backup folder? Your recovery key file alone brings your identity back; your spaces return from the people you share them with.

Verdict rules

  • Protected (green): backup succeeded within 10 days and the key is in the folder.
  • At risk (amber): not set up, or set up but the passphrase was never confirmed on this computer. A missing copy of the recovery key does not change the verdict — it stays a tip.
  • Stopped (red): no backup for 10 days, or the key is missing from the folder.
  • Devices without a system keychain: the identity row says so and the verdict cannot be green without a backup.

Rejected

  • One screen for status and settings (what #585 has now): breaks the app's rule that Profile shows the device and Settings configures it.
  • A “Security” section in Settings: there is nothing to configure about the keychain; it is a fact about this device.
  • “Second copy” wording: does not say what is copied. It is the recovery key — the user's identity — so the row says so.
